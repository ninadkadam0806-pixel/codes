Kaho Naa... Pyaar Hai

A beautiful romantic musical film released in 2000.

About the Movie

Kaho Naa... Pyaar Hai is a romantic movie film directed by Rakesh Roshan. It features Hrithik
Roshan and Ameesha Patel in the lead roles.

The movie was released on 14 January 2000. It became famous for its songs,romance, and
memorable performances.


Genre: Romance,Musical,Thriller
Language: Hindi
Release Year: 2000

Story Summary

Rohit is a young singer who falls in love with Sonia. Their love story takes an unexpected turn after a tragic incident. The story continues with mystery, romance, and surprising discoveries.

Love, music, and destiny make this movie memorable.
